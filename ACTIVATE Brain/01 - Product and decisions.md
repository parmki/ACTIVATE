# Product and decisions

- Today, Tasks, Habits, Goals, Insights.
- Prioritize a fast checklist, clear progress, and an uncluttered experience.
- Tasks may have dates and times, but neither is required.
- Habits support daily and selected weekdays; a missed scheduled day breaks a streak, but today is not missed until it ends.
- Each completed task or habit check-in contributes one activity square count. No XP or effort scores.
- User requested ACTIVATE, not the older placeholder project name.
- Local storage is a practical first step, not an account system or a backup.
- The profile is local to the device and supports display name, short description, appearance, and JSON backup/restore. It is not a login.
- The ACTIVATE wordmark is a custom geometric SVG with a blue bolt beside the wordmark. Light and dark variants are in the public folder and do not depend on a font download.
- Use restrained feedback and honor the operating system's reduced-motion preference.
